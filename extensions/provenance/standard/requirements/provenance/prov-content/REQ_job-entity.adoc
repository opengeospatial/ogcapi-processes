[[req_provenance_job-prov_relation-job-entity]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/provenance/prov-content-job-entity

[.component,class=part]
--
The PROV metadata SHALL provide a link:https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/#Entity[`prov:Entity`] definition
to represent the job status information, which reflects the state of the associated job execution.
This PROV Job Status Entity is distinct from the <<req_provenance_prov-content_job-activity,PROV Job Activity>>
that represents the execution itself.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
The PROV entity corresponding to the job status SHALL be related to the
<<req_provenance_prov-content_job-activity,PROV Job Activity>> using either the
link:https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/#wasInfluencedBy[`prov:wasInfluencedBy`] relationship
or its derived link:https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/#wasGeneratedBy[`prov:wasGeneratedBy`] relationship.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
When using `prov:wasInfluencedBy`, the `prov:influencee` SHALL refer to the PROV Job Status Entity
and the `prov:influencer` SHALL refer to the PROV Job Activity.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
When using `prov:wasGeneratedBy`, the `prov:entity` SHALL refer to the PROV Job Status Entity
and the `prov:activity` SHALL refer to the PROV Job Activity.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
The PROV entity corresponding to the job status SHALL include
a `prov:location` attribute with the absolute URI of the job status (i.e.: `{root}/jobs/{jobID}`) as value.
--

====

NOTE: Both relationships are allowed to let the server reflect the nuance about how it concretely manages
the creation of the job status. The `prov:wasInfluencedBy` association is a broader relation of `prov:wasGeneratedBy`.
The `prov:wasInfluencedBy` relation can represent, for example, when the job status is a reflection of the job activity
that is produced by the API without being directly generated by the job execution activity itself. On the other hand,
the `prov:wasGeneratedBy` explicitly indicates that the job execution activity concretely generated the job status.
The applicable one is left up to the discretion of the server implementation.
