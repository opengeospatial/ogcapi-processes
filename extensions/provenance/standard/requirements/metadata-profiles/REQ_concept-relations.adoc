[[req_metadata-profiles_concept-relations]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/metadata-profiles/concept-relations

[.component,class=part]
--
The concept of the `href` SHALL be related to the concept of the `role` by at least one explicit semantic relation,
rather than by loosely coupled definitions.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
The semantic relation SHALL be resolvable by dereferencing the `href` concept, the `role` concept, or any concept
of a chain of related concepts leading from one to the other.
--
