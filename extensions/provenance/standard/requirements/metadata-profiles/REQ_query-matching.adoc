[[req_metadata-profiles_query-matching]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/metadata-profiles/query-matching

[.component,class=part]
--
A `<role>` SHALL be matched against the `role` of `metadata` field entries irrespective of the identifiers (`id`)
of the process, inputs or outputs where they are defined.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
When many `<profile>` are provided for a same `<role>`, the process SHALL match ALL of them (logical AND).
--

[.component,class=part]
--
Each `<profile>` SHALL be matched against the `href` of a
<<req_metadata-profiles_profile-link,_Metadata Profile Link_>> with that `role`.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
For `inputs` and `outputs`, each `<profile>` of a given `<role>` SHALL be matched by at least one input
(respectively output) of the process.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
For `inputs` and `outputs`, a match SHALL NOT require the profiles of a same `<role>` to be provided by the same input
(respectively output).
--

[.component,class=part]
--
When multiple `<role>` are provided, each `<role>` SHALL be evaluated separately.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
For `inputs` and `outputs`, a match SHALL NOT require distinct `<role>` to be provided by the same input
(respectively output).
--

[.component,class=part]
--
When multiple `<role>` are provided, the process SHALL match ALL of them to be returned.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
A process matching only a subset of the requested roles SHALL NOT be returned.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
A server SHALL NOT ignore a supplied `<role>`.
The filter is evaluated as a whole, and results in a match or not.
--

====
