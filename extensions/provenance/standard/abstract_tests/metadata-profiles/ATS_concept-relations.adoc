[[ats_metadata-profiles_concept-relations]]

[abstract_test]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /conf/metadata-profiles/concept-relations
target:: /req/metadata-profiles/concept-relations
test-purpose:: Validate that the `href` concept of a profile is explicitly related to the `role` concept.
test-method::
+
--
1. Retrieve the `role` and `href` URIs of profile metadata entries from the process descriptions.

2. Dereference the `href` concept, the `role` concept, and any intermediate related concept (when resolvable).

3. Validate that a chain of explicit semantic relations (e.g., `rdfs:subClassOf`, `owl:equivalentClass`, `owl:sameAs`,
`skos:exactMatch`, `skos:closeMatch`, `skos:broader`, `skos:narrower`) leads from the `href` concept to the `role` concept.
--
====
