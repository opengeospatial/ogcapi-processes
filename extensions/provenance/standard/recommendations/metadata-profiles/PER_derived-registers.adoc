[[per_metadata-profiles_derived-registers]]
[permission]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /per/metadata-profiles/derived-registers

[.component,class=part]
--
The `href` of a <<req_metadata-profiles_profile-link,profile link>> MAY identify a concept defined externally from
the register of the `role` concept, in order to extend a base profile with concepts of other registers, while still
being related to the `role` concept as required by
<<req_metadata-profiles_role-register,/req/metadata-profiles/role-register>>
and as described by <<req_metadata-profiles_concept-relations,/req/metadata-profiles/concept-relations>>.
--
