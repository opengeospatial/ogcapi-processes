[[rec_metadata-profiles_role-registers]]
[recommendation]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /rec/metadata-profiles/role-registers

[.component,class=part]
--
The `role` URI SHOULD be selected from the definitions of the
link:https://defs.opengis.net/prez/catalogs/ogc-cat:register/col/registers:bblocks[OGC Building Blocks registers]
or of the link:https://www.opengis.net/def/profile[OGC profiles register].
--

[.component,class=part]
--
The `role` URI SHOULD be dereferenceable to a registered definition of its concept.
--

[.component,class=part]
--
The `href` URI SHOULD be a concept registered under the register referred to by the `role`.
--

====

NOTE: Dereferencing of URIs is not a requirement for security reasons. It is also allowed for ontologies still being
worked on that are not fully published and validated by their owning authorities. Servers can resolve those URIs as
plain string matching as deemed fit. However, valid links are still recommended even if not accessed by the server
to help discoverability of their concepts by other entities that might have access to external networks, and to
facilitate interpretation and exploration of its <<def_knowledge-graph,_knowledge graph_>> concept relationships.
